Methodology

We start with public bestseller pages on Amazon, TikTok Shop and Meta Shops, then cross-check review velocity, listing age, stock status and third-party sales estimates. Where platforms publish no sales data, we model a range from review count and category velocity, then take the midpoint.

The result is an estimated 30-day unit figure. It is directional, not audited. We refresh the list monthly and keep a changelog. No brand can pay for placement; affiliate links, where present, never affect rank.

Important caveat

Amazon, Meta and TikTok do not publish official category sales rankings. Every unit number here is an estimate. Treat the order as a useful map, not a receipt.

What we do not do

We do not use private seller data, scrape logged-in dashboards, or accept payment for placement. Sponsored products are not in this index.
